    The rules it runs by
    • Nothing publishes without approval from you or your staff.
    • Every post and ad follows medical-board, FTC and platform advertising rules.
    • Patient photos and stories are used only with written consent.
    • AI-generated images are never presented as real results.
    • It never gives medical advice or diagnoses. Clinical questions go to a person.
    • HIPAA groundwork before any patient data goes in: encryption, access logs and a compliance review.
